SimpleBible privacy policy

Last updated: 17 November 2026

What the app collects

Nothing. SimpleBible has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no crash reporting. It does not collect, store on a server, sell or share any personal data.

What stays on your phone

Your notes, bookmarks, highlights, reading progress and settings are stored only on your device. They are never sent anywhere. Backups are files you choose to save, wherever you choose to save them. Uninstalling the app or clearing its storage deletes everything.

When the app goes online

Only when you ask it to:

  • downloading a translation you pick, from eBible.org;
  • opening the page of an ancient manuscript, from the library or research institute that holds the photos (for example the Internet Archive, the Vatican Library, Cambridge University Library, or the INTF in Münster);
  • opening a link you tap, such as Wikipedia or an online edition, in your browser.

Those sites receive the ordinary information any web request carries (such as your IP address), under their own privacy policies. SimpleBible adds nothing about you to these requests.

Children

The app collects no data from anyone, including children.

Contact

Questions about this policy: simplebible@outlook.com.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.
